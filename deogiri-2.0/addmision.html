Admissions 2025–26

Join Deogiri College – where knowledge meets innovation. Admissions now open for UG, PG, and Diploma programs.

Available Programs

  • 🎓 Bachelor of Arts (B.A.)
  • 💻 Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.)
  • 💼 Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.)
  • ⚙️ Master of Science (M.Sc.)
  • 📘 Diploma and Certificate Courses

Eligibility Criteria

  • For UG Courses: 10+2 (HSC) from recognized board with relevant subjects.
  • For PG Courses: Graduation in respective stream with minimum 50% marks.
  • Diploma Courses: Open for all streams with minimum 10th pass.

Admission Process

  • Step 1: Visit the official Deogiri College portal.
  • Step 2: Register and fill in the online admission form.
  • Step 3: Upload required documents and pay application fee.
  • Step 4: Wait for merit list publication and counseling call.

Important Dates

  • 📅 Admission Form Opens: June 10, 2025
  • 📅 Last Date for Submission: July 15, 2025
  • 📅 Merit List Announcement: July 25, 2025
  • 📅 Classes Begin: August 5, 2025
